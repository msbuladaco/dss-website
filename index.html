About

Who we are

The Division of Soil Science is one of the divisions of the Agricultural Systems Institute (ASI), College of Agriculture and Food Science, University of the Philippines Los Baños. Our faculty, researchers and technical staff teach soil science from introductory principles to advanced soil fertility, soil physics and soil microbiology, and mentor undergraduate and graduate students.

Our research spans soil chemistry and fertility, nutrient recycling, pedology and land-use suitability, soil biology and organic amendments, and the management of heavy metals and other contaminants for food safety. We work with national and international partners including DOST-PCAARRD, DA-BAR, ACIAR, VLIR-UOS and DOST-JSPS.

Through the Analytical Service Laboratory and soil test kit services, we also provide soil and plant analyses to UPLB and other clients.
